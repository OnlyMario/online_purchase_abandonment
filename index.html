03 / RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Turning Behavior Into Insights.

Our research question: What factors influence whether an online shopper completes or abandons a purchase?

01 / COLLECT

Customer Data

Gather publicly available online shopping session data to examine customer activity and purchasing outcomes.

02 / ANALYZE

Behavior Patterns

Compare visitor types, product-related activity, shopping sessions, and revenue outcomes through descriptive analysis.

03 / PRESENT

Data Visualization

Use interactive charts and tables to communicate patterns and explore predictive classification if supported by the dataset.

PLANNED DATA SOURCE

UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset

This dataset includes shopping session attributes and a Revenue outcome. It does not directly identify abandoned shopping carts.
